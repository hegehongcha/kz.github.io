I am a Ph.D. candidate in **Electrical and Computer Engineering** at the **University of Pittsburgh**, advised by [Prof. Liang Zhan](https://www.engineering.pitt.edu/people/faculty/liang-zhan/). I also collaborate closely with [Prof. Chenghua Lin](https://chenghua-lin.github.io/) at the University of Manchester.

I develop **LLM & NLP** methods for evaluating open-ended text generation and improving post-training, alongside **Medical AI** methods for evidence-grounded multimodal generation and interpretable imaging–genomics modeling.

## Research

<div class="research-grid">
  <section class="research-card">
    <span class="track-label">Research track 1</span>
    <h3>LLM &amp; NLP</h3>
    <p><strong>Reliable evaluation.</strong> Modeling multiple valid responses (CMN), combining specialized small models with LLMs (SLIDE and DRE), and using structured semantics through AMR.</p>
    <p><strong>Structured feedback for post-training.</strong> Developing fine-grained credit assignment for generated outputs with distinct semantic roles; HERO instantiates this general problem in radiology.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="research-card">
    <span class="track-label">Research track 2</span>
    <h3>Medical AI</h3>
    <p><strong>Evidence-grounded radiology generation.</strong> Improving consistency among visual evidence, clinical findings, and diagnostic summaries through semantic evaluation and RL post-training.</p>
    <p><strong>Interpretable multimodal learning.</strong> Integrating 3D MRI, genetic data, and brain-network structure for clinically meaningful prediction and analysis.</p>
  </section>
</div>

<div class="bridge-note"><strong>Bridge project · HERO.</strong> A general method for structured credit assignment in RL post-training, studied through evidence-grounded radiology generation.</div>
